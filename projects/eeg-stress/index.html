EEG Stress Identification System

Classifying mental state as stressed or relaxed by processing EEG signals in LabVIEW. Presented at ISC-2021.

Overview

A biosignal-analysis project using EEG acquisition and LabVIEW to investigate patterns associated with stress identification. The system processes electroencephalography (EEG) signals to identify whether a subject's mental state is stressed or relaxed.

Approach

EEG signals were acquired and processed in LabVIEW, applying signal-processing steps to extract patterns associated with stressed versus relaxed mental states and classify the subject's state accordingly.

Presentation

This work was presented at the ISC-2021 conference.
