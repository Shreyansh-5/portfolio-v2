How I approach a problem

  1. Start with function.Define the human need, environment and meaningful outcome before choosing technology.
  2. Measure the right signal.Build the sensing and evidence plan around a defensible question.
  3. Design for translation.Consider usability, safety and regulatory constraints while the system is still flexible.
  4. Communicate across disciplines.Make engineering decisions legible to clinical, quality and research teams.
